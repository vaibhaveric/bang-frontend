Privacy Policy

Bangali Sweets & Dryfruits respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you shop with us, why we collect it, how we keep it safe, and the choices and rights you have over it.

1 Who we are & the scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy is issued by Bangali Sweets & Dryfruits (“we”, “us”, “our”), a sweet, bakery and dry-fruit business operating from Pustak Bazar, Mahveer Ganj, Kiratpura, Bhind, Madhya Pradesh 477001, India.

It applies to all personal information we handle when you visit bangalisweets.in, place an order online or by phone/WhatsApp, sign in to an account, join our Mithai Points programme, request corporate or wedding catering, or visit our physical outlet. By using our website or services, you consent to the practices described here.

2 Information we collect

We only collect information that helps us bake, pack, deliver and improve your order. This includes:

Information you give us

  • Contact & identity details — your name, mobile number, and (if provided) email address.
  • Delivery information — shipping and billing addresses, recipient names, pin code, and any delivery notes you add.
  • Order details — items purchased, quantities, gift messages, custom-cake instructions, and dietary or allergen requests.
  • Account data — your saved addresses, order history, and Mithai Points balance, all linked to your verified phone number.
  • Business details — for B2B, corporate gifting or GST invoicing: company name, GSTIN, and authorised contact.
  • Communications — messages you send us over WhatsApp, phone, email, or web forms, including photos submitted for freshness/quality claims.

Information we collect automatically

  • Device & usage data — IP address, browser type, device, pages viewed, and the cart/checkout actions you take, stored to keep the site working and secure.
  • Local storage — your cart contents and sign-in session are saved in your browser so you don't lose them between visits.

We do not collect or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, or net-banking credentials. Those are handled directly by our payment partner (see Section 5).

3 How we use your information

We use your personal information to:

  • Prepare, pack, and deliver your sweets, bakery and dry-fruit orders to the correct address.
  • Verify your identity at sign-in using a one-time password (OTP) sent to your phone.
  • Send order confirmations, dispatch alerts, delivery updates, and pickup-ready notifications by SMS, WhatsApp, or email.
  • Process payments, issue GST-compliant invoices, and handle refunds or re-shipments.
  • Operate our Mithai Points loyalty programme and apply your discounts.
  • Respond to your enquiries, quality claims, and catering or corporate-gifting requests.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, or misuse of the site.
  • Comply with food-safety (FSSAI), tax (GST), and other legal obligations.
  • With your consent, send occasional festive offers and new-product news — which you can opt out of at any time.

4 When and why we share information

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted parties who help us run the business, and only to the extent needed:

  • Delivery & logistics partners (e.g. Delhivery, Bluedart, local riders) — receive the recipient's name, address, and phone number to complete delivery.
  • Payment gateways — receive the transaction amount and order reference to process your payment securely.
  • Communication providers — SMS/WhatsApp gateways that send your OTPs and order updates.
  • Professional advisors & authorities — accountants, auditors, or government bodies where required by law, court order, or a valid legal request.

Where we use third parties, we require them to protect your data and use it only for the purpose we engaged them for.

5 Payments & security

All online payments are processed over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection through a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We never see or store your full card or UPI credentials.

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards — encryption in transit, access controls, and staff confidentiality — to protect your information against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep access to your phone and OTPs private, as these are used to access your account.

6 Cookies & tracking

We use a small number of cookies and browser local-storage items to keep the site working — for example, to remember your cart, keep you signed in, and understand which pages are popular so we can improve them. We may use privacy-respecting analytics to count visits in aggregate.

You can clear or block cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Doing so may mean your cart or session is not remembered between visits.

7 How long we keep your data

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law:

  • Order & invoice records — retained for the period required under GST and Indian tax/accounting law (generally up to 8 years).
  • Account & Mithai Points data — kept while your account is active. Points expire 1 year from the date earned.
  • Marketing preferences — kept until you opt out.

When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

8 Your rights & choices

Subject to applicable Indian law, you may:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated details (you can edit most of these in your account).
  • Delete your account and associated personal data, where we are not legally required to retain it.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing messages at any time, without affecting your orders.
  • Object to a particular use of your data, or ask us to restrict it.

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time and may verify your identity (via your registered phone number) before acting on a request.

9 Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has provided us information without parental consent, please contact us and we will delete it. Orders placed on behalf of a household are the responsibility of the adult account holder.

10 Changes to this policy & how to contact us

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or the law. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest revision. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page. Your continued use of our services after an update means you accept the revised policy.

For any privacy question, request, or complaint, please reach out — we read every message.
